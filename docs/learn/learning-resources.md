# Learning resources

Free books and courses.

## R

- [Geocomputation with R](https://r.geocompx.org/) covers sf, terra and tmap.
- [Spatial Data Science with applications in R](https://r-spatial.org/book/) by Edzer Pebesma and Roger Bivand.
- [Spatial Data Science with R and terra](https://rspatial.org/) by Robert Hijmans.

## Python

- [Geocomputation with Python](https://py.geocompx.org/) covers GeoPandas, rasterio and shapely.
- [Geographic Data Science with Python](https://geographicdata.science/book/) by Sergio Rey, Dani Arribas-Bel and Levi Wolf.
- [Python for Geographic Data Analysis](https://pythongis.org/) from the University of Helsinki.

## Julia

- [Geospatial Data Science with Julia](https://juliaearth.github.io/geospatial-data-science-with-julia/) by Júlio Hoffimann.

## Desktop GIS

- [QGIS Training Manual](https://docs.qgis.org/latest/en/docs/training_manual/index.html) from the QGIS project.

## Earth observation

- [Earth Data Science](https://www.earthdatascience.org/) from Earth Lab at the University of Colorado Boulder.

## Communities

- [OSGeo](https://www.osgeo.org/) hosts open source geospatial projects. [FOSS4G](https://foss4g.org/) is its annual conference.
- [OGC](https://www.ogc.org/) develops geospatial standards such as WMS, WFS and GeoPackage.
